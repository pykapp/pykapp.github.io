*people you know* is end-to-end encrypted. This page says what that means
for moderation, plainly, because it is not what most apps mean.

**Last updated:** 7 September 2026.

## What we cannot do

We cannot see photos, captions, comments, names or album titles. They are
encrypted on the phone that made them, with keys we never hold. So we do not
and cannot scan content, filter it, or review it. There is no automated
system looking at your photographs, because there is nothing for one to look
at. The same is true for a report: when you report a photo, we do not receive
the photo.

## What a report contains

When you report a post, a comment or a person, we receive:

- who filed the report;
- what it points at, and which account that thing belongs to;
- when;
- what you wrote in the box, in the clear.

That last line is the one thing about a report we can read, so please say
what is wrong in words. We never receive a copy of the content, and we never
tell the person reported who reported them, or that a report exists.

## What we can do

Everything we can do acts on an account, never on a photograph:

- **Dismiss** the report.
- **Suspend** the account. A suspended account can still sign in, look at
  what it already has, export its data and delete itself, but cannot post,
  comment, react, or send requests. A suspension can be lifted.
- **Delete** the account, which is the same deletion the person could have
  done themselves: their posts, comments and reactions go for everyone at
  once, and their connections are severed.

We cannot take down one photograph and leave the account. We have no key to
tell one from another, and half a moderation would be worse than an honest
one.

## What you can do yourself, at once

The strongest tools are yours, and they do not wait for us:

- **Remove** somebody. Both of you stop seeing anything the other shared.
- **Block** somebody. Everything remove does, and they cannot find you, ask
  again, or reach you through an album a third person made.
- **Delete** a post of yours for everyone.
- **Delete** a comment somebody left on your post.

Because there are no strangers, no public profiles and no discovery, the
people who can reach you are people you chose, and the way to stop somebody
is to unchoose them.

## Limits

Reporting is rate-limited so that the queue cannot be flooded. A report
about something you cannot see is refused the same way the thing itself
would be. We do not accept reports about albums, because an album is a
room: report the photograph in it, or the person.
